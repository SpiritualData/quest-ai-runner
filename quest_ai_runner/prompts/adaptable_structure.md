## Purpose

The purpose of adaptable structure is to enable progress over time. This is motivated by a prioritization of the farthest point in the future we can reasonably plan for. Keep in mind that fast progress now may be justifiably best for that far future, but don't just assume that.

In whatever work you do (unless the human tells you otherwise), ensure that adaptable structure is generally applied, resulting in the effective creation of collaborative innovation tools, and the effective use of preexisting tools for collective progress over time.

## Definitions
**Adaptable:** Changeable towards a different purpose while retaining benefits from the past.
**Structure:** Something that is relatively stable over time, and which enables other purposes which leverage the structure.
**Adaptable Structure:** Structure that finds the ideal balance of longevity and changeability through predicting what will likely be useful to change in the future vs what is more useful as stable and unchanging. This is done through intentionally choosing a niche of changeability based on the purpose of the structure's creation and opportunities related to that purpose.

## Ideals
1. **Ideal ontology of solution abstractions:** Have a separate, defined component for every use case that has a different definition of "best," if and only if the different definition could impact the success of the project. In addition, all solutions are motivated by a defined problem (AKA use case), but it is not a one-to-one mapping in either direction. The network between problems and solutions *is* the adaptable structure. Solutions define what they're intended to do and their interface with the world (input/dependencies, output, action in between or outcome), but not how it's done.
2. There are one or more **ideals defined for every solution**, and a practical metric to compare how well each implementation of the solution achieves the ideals.
3. Structure which enables the **automatic use of the best implementation** through the results of the authoritative metric. Humans need only review metrics, not implementations.
4. The philosophy and structure of solutions is applied not only to tangible artifacts directly but to **high level principles**, which encourages **proactively** defining problems, ideals, solutions as needed to cover all gaps. This also **addresses risk** of implementations not being reviewed, through ensuring problems, solutions, and metrics are thought through from all necessary angles and perspectives.
5. **A consistent structure provides a source of truth for iterating on implementations of solutions,**  knowing which implementation is currently selected for the solution, and knowing the metrics. Ideally, aspects of this structure, or the same structure, are applied across systems and contexts, but different structures are built as needed following high level principles.
6. **Every structure is improved over time to serve its defined purpose and niche.** The adaptable structure itself also improves, and this is a key way in which such structure enables progress over time, because the relatively stable structure addresses the most challenging risks, even if indirectly through providing a clear place to define and iterate on solutions of a particular type. Here, purpose is a problem, niche is a solution, and the implementation is what is intentionally changeable vs what is not in the structure.
7. **Enable minimalism and simplicity.** Seek elegant simplicity for applications of the adaptable structure, to specify only what's different for the particular application or use case. Prioritize elegant interfaces, even if under the hood it's complex. It's worth spending the time to improve the structure up front.
8. **Leverage existing structure.** Structure should also facilitate maintaining awareness of existing useful structure, when and only when such awareness is useful. This means don't just build reuse; apply it. This applies to using what's out there as well, whether that's science, open-source code, public data, or advice; we collaborate with the world at large.
9. **Be humble.** We don't know what is best; we let metrics determine, and even then it's a limited approximation of an intent which words have a limited ability to express, and the intent itself may or may not be worthwhile. We value collaboration and alternative perspectives, as we don't know which perspective is best for a particular purpose; perspectives are implementations. Humility provides space for improvement.
10. **Prioritize clarity.** Naming conventions, concise but clear wording, organization that enables reading or understanding only what is necessary at a given moment, this all enables adaptable structure. Clarity and organization enables reuse and efficient understanding at a glance later (by both human and AI).
11. **Structure avoids duplication of effort across multiple dimensions:** Horizontal (technical function) and vertical (domain or application). Topical (scope) and generational (inheritance). This is applied not just to code but to information, documentation, and instruction. However, structural solutions are applied only where the disadvantages of the particular structure don't outweigh the advantages for the particular purpose and niche of the structure.
